The case for a recruitment partner

The right hire
changes everything.

You're building something that matters. But hiring in Climatech, Advanced Materials, and Industry 4.0 isn't like hiring in big tech: the talent pools are smaller, the stakes are higher, and a wrong hire costs more than just a salary.

Six reasons to work with a specialist recruiter
Access Passive Candidates
The best people in your space aren't on job boards. They're heads-down building. A recruiter with deep domain networks reaches the candidates you can't find on LinkedIn.
Full-Time Recruiting Without the Full-Time Cost
At the pre-seed through Series C stage, hiring comes in waves: three critical roles one quarter, nothing the next. A full-time internal recruiter is a six-figure fixed cost you can't justify year-round. A recruitment partner gives you that firepower exactly when you need it, and disappears when you don't.
Reduce the Cost of a Bad Hire
A mis-hire at an early-stage company doesn't just cost salary. It costs momentum. The U.S. Department of Labor estimates a bad hire runs up to 30% of their first-year earnings. When your team is 15 people, one wrong call is felt everywhere.
Market Intelligence
A specialist recruiter knows what competitors are paying, where talent is concentrated, and what's motivating candidates to move. That intel shapes better offers and faster closes.
Speed to Hire
Every day a critical role sits open costs momentum. A recruiter with an active network can deliver qualified candidates in days, not months, because the outreach started before your search did.
Confidentiality & Discretion
Replacing a key leader? Entering a new market? Some searches can't be public. A recruiter runs the process discreetly, protecting your strategy and your team's stability.

of searches
The average recruitment firm abandons nearly 3 out of 4 searches before making a placement. At VoltForce, we operate with a 100% fill rate mentality: we don't quit until the work is done.
