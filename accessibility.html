Accessibility

Accessibility Statement

Last updated: July 28, 2026

VoltForce LLC is committed to making its website usable by as many people as possible, including people with disabilities. This statement explains where we stand and how to reach us if something is not working for you.

Our commitment

We want everyone to be able to learn about our services and apply for roles, regardless of ability or the technology they use. We treat accessibility as ongoing work rather than a one-time task.

Measures we take

  • We aim to follow widely recognized accessibility guidelines, specifically the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA, as a goal.
  • We consider accessibility as we design and update pages, including readable contrast, clear structure, and keyboard navigability.
  • We review and improve accessibility over time as we make changes to the site.

Conformance status

We are working toward the WCAG 2.1 AA guidelines but we do not claim that the entire site is fully conformant. Some content, including certain third-party content or documents, may not yet meet every guideline. We welcome feedback that helps us improve.

Feedback and help

If you have trouble using any part of our site, or you need information in a different format, please contact us at hello@volt-force.com. Tell us the page and the problem, and we will do our best to help you and to fix the issue. If you are applying for a role and need a reasonable accommodation, let us know and we will assist you and coordinate with the employer as appropriate.
