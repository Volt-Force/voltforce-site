02 · AI Fit

Every candidate, scored for fit.

As candidates come in, Filament ATS checks each full resume against your must-have criteria and job description, and puts a percentage fit right on their card. Your team starts with the people worth a conversation.

Resume + Must-Haves

A fit score on every new candidate.

The score weighs the full resume against the must-haves you set and the job description you wrote: level, hands-on depth, and real evidence of the work, not keyword overlap. Careers page applicants and the candidates your team adds are scored the same way, automatically, and the score sits right on each card in Sourced, your first look.

You Set the Bar

Your must-haves. Your call.

Add must-haves and nice-to-haves to any role you run. A candidate missing a must-have is flagged, and a nice-to-have adds to the score without ruling anyone out. Edit the role and live candidates are scored again. The score is a head start. The green, yellow, or red call stays with your team.

03 · Your Careers Page

Publish a role. It's live.

Filament ATS includes a careers page under your name and your brand color. Publish a role and it is live. Every application lands straight in that role's pipeline.

Under Your Brand

Candidates apply to you.

Your company name, your accent color, and a shareable page for every role, ready for LinkedIn, email, or anywhere else. Publish or pull a role whenever you like, grouped by department. Applications land in the pipeline, with no inbox to forward and nothing to retype.

04 · Your Team

Everyone sees what they should.

Add teammates and hiring managers yourself, and decide what each person can see and do. Admins run the workspace. Hiring managers stay focused on their own roles.

Per-person permissions, set by your admins

Roles + Permissions

The right view for every person.

Admins manage users, departments, and settings, and see every role. Teammates work the pipeline within the permissions an admin grants. Hiring managers see only the roles they are assigned to. Remove someone and their access is gone.

Compensation + Reporting

Sensitive data, only where it belongs.

Salary and offer details sit behind their own per-person permission. Admins decide exactly who can see or record compensation, and everyone else works the pipeline without it. Reporting is built in: your hiring funnel and time in each stage, role by role.
