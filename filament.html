Applicant Tracking · Now in Beta

Switch on your hiring.

Filament ATS is an applicant tracking system for technical hiring teams. Your roles, your pipeline, your candidates, your careers page, and your whole hiring team, working from the same live view. Your team runs it. Your data stays yours.

The Workflow

See every hire in full light.

Open a role, collect applicants, move them through your stages, and make the call, all in one place. Every candidate, every stage, and every note sits on one record your whole team can see.

Jobs + Pipeline

A pipeline board for every role.

Create and edit roles, organize them by department, and assign each one to the hiring managers who own it. Every role gets its own board, Sourced through Hired. Move candidates, leave notes, and rate them as interviews happen.

One board
Per role, Sourced through Hired
  • Roles organized by department
  • Hiring managers assigned per role
  • Stage moves, notes, and a green, yellow, or red call on each candidate
  • Every hire recorded as a placement
Candidate Pool

Your candidates, searchable to the resume.

Applicants, people your team adds, and anyone you bring over from your old system live in one pool that belongs to you. Search it down to resume text, so the strong runner-up from last spring is one search away when the next role opens.

Your pool
Built by your team, owned by your team
  • Every applicant kept on record
  • Add candidates your team finds
  • Searchable down to resume text
  • Bring your existing candidates across
Careers Page

Publish a role. It's live.

Filament ATS includes a careers page under your company name and your brand color, with its own page for every role. Publish a role and it is live. Every application lands straight in that role's pipeline, with no inbox to forward and nothing to retype.

Your brand
Candidates apply to you, not to a job board
  • Your company name and accent color
  • A shareable page for every role
  • Publish or pull a role whenever you like
  • Applications land in the pipeline
Your Team

Everyone sees what they should.

Add teammates and hiring managers yourself, and decide what each person can see and do. Admins run the workspace. Hiring managers stay focused on their own roles.

Roles + Permissions

The right view for every person.

Admins manage users, departments, and settings, and see every role. Teammates work the pipeline within the permissions an admin grants. Hiring managers see only the roles they are assigned to. Remove someone and their access is gone.

3 Roles
Admin · Teammate · Hiring Manager
  • Admins run the workspace
  • Teammates work the pipeline
  • Hiring managers see their own roles
  • Access ends when you say
Compensation + Reporting

Sensitive data, only where it belongs.

Salary and offer details sit behind their own per-person permission. Admins decide exactly who can see or record compensation, and everyone else works the pipeline without it. Reporting is built in: your hiring funnel and time in each stage, role by role.

Need-to-know
Compensation is its own permission
  • Per-person compensation access
  • Hiring funnel for every role
  • Time in each stage
  • Every placement on record
Your Data

Your data. Yours alone.

Candidate data is confidential, and the people in your pipeline trust you with it. Filament ATS is built so it stays yours.

Self-Hosted + Isolated

Self-hosted and walled off.

Filament runs on infrastructure VoltForce owns and operates end to end, so no third-party SaaS vendor holds your pipeline. Every company's workspace is isolated from every other, and an automated isolation test runs before every release. A release that fails it does not ship.

Isolated
Tested before every release
  • Self-hosted infrastructure
  • No third-party SaaS vendor in between
  • Every workspace walled off
  • Isolation test gates every release
Our Commitment

Your pipeline stays yours.

Filament is built by VoltForce, which also runs a recruiting practice. So we say it plainly: your pipeline, your applicants, and your careers page submissions are never used to source candidates for anyone else.

Never mined
Built that way from day one
  • Your applicants stay your applicants
  • Never used to source for anyone else
  • Your own isolated workspace
  • Confidential by design
