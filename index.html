Introducing Filament · Now in Beta

Your platform. Your recruiter.
One system.

Filament is an all-in-one hiring platform: sourcing, outreach, applicant tracking, careers page, and client portal in one system. It comes with a recruiter. It doesn't require one. Every client gets a live, end-to-end view of their pipeline, from day one through placement closing.

Hiring Infrastructure

Part of Your Hiring Stack

For Talent Partner clients, Filament becomes part of your hiring stack. A client-branded careers page, a real-time portal for your team, and an embedded function with the depth of a sector-specialist firm.

One Shared System

Same Data, Same Moment

Client and recruiter work in the same system, on the same data, at the same time. Every candidate, every stage, every note, the moment it happens. Self-hosted on infrastructure VoltForce owns and operates end-to-end. Your data, yours alone.

Meet the Founder

Matt Anders

Founder, VoltForce

"After nearly a decade recruiting across Climatech and deeptech at some of the most well-known firms in the space, one thing was obvious: great hiring looks completely different when the platform and the recruiter are the same system.

Candidates deserve to be treated like people. Clients deserve to see the work while it is happening, not summarised after the fact.

I knew there had to be a better way.

We're here to help build the future, and we're damn good at it."
